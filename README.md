Fatima's Spoken English Centre Website
A simple, point-by-point guide to what this website does and how it works.

1. What This Website Is For
   •	Lets visitors see Fatima's spoken-English courses and sign up online.
   •	Removes the need for Fatima to answer every enquiry call herself.
   •	Gives Fatima one private dashboard to manage courses and confirm students.
3. Who Uses It
   •	Visitors — people browsing courses and filling the enrolment form (no login needed).
   •	Fatima (Admin) — the only person who can manage courses and view/confirm enrolments, through a private login.
4. Public Website — What a Visitor Sees
   1.	Course Listing Page
       •	Shows every active course: name, start date, and what's taught / what the student gets.
       •	Shows seats left for each course (e.g. "5 spots left").
       •	A course automatically disappears from the public list once it's full — no manual work needed from Fatima.
2.	Enrolment Form
       •	Visitor picks a course and fills in: Name, Gender, Age, Phone Number, Email.
       •	Form won't accept blank or obviously fake details.
       •	Visitor must verify their phone/email with an OTP before the form is accepted — this stops fake/spam entries.
       •	Once verified, the enrolment is saved and a seat is provisionally counted against that course's limit.
3.	Confirmation
   •	After OTP verification, visitor sees a message like: "Thanks! Your spot is being reviewed — you'll get a confirmation email shortly."
   •	Visitor does not get told they have a confirmed seat yet — that only happens after Fatima accepts them (see below).
4. Admin Side — What Fatima Sees
   1.	Admin Signup (one-time only)
      •	The very first time, Fatima creates her own admin account (email + password).
      •	After that, signup is closed — only login is available. No one else can create an admin account.
2.	Admin Login
   •	Fatima logs in with her email + password to reach her private dashboard.
   •	Nothing in the admin area is visible to the public.
3.	Course Management
   •	Fatima can add, edit, or deactivate courses.
   •	For each course she sets: name, start date, course content (what's taught / what student gets), and total seat limit.
   •	The seats-left count updates automatically as students enrol.
4.	Enrolments Dashboard
   •	Fatima sees a list of everyone who filled the form and passed OTP verification: Name, Gender, Age, Phone, Email, Course, submitted date/time.
   •	Next to each entry is an Accept button and a Remove button.
   •	When Fatima clicks Accept:
       ◦	That student is marked as confirmed.
       ◦	The student automatically receives a confirmation email for their seat.
       ◦	Fatima does not need to call the student to confirm anymore — the accept button replaces that call.
   •	When Fatima clicks Remove:
       ◦	That student's enrolment is deleted from the list.
       ◦	Their seat is freed up again (seats-left count goes back up, and the course reappears publicly if it had become full).
       ◦	Useful for removing no-shows, duplicate entries, or people who cancel.
   •	Fatima can still see who hasn't been accepted yet, so she knows who's pending.
5.	Account Management
   •	The first admin (the one who originally signed up — e.g. Fatima) has an option in her account settings to remove/delete her own admin account.
   •	Once she removes her account, signup opens back up again, so a new owner can create a fresh admin account and take over managing the site.
   •	This is meant for situations like the business changing hands — it's a deliberate, one-way action (she'd need to confirm before it happens, since it removes her access immediately).
5. How Seats Work, Step by Step
   1.	Fatima sets a seat limit when creating a course (e.g. 20 seats for Beginner batch).
   2.	Every OTP-verified enrolment counts against that limit and reduces "seats left" on the public page.
   3.	When seats left reaches 0, the course is automatically hidden from the public course listing — no new enrolments can come in for it.
   4.	Fatima can still see and manage a full course from her admin dashboard (e.g. to review pending enrolments or reopen seats if needed).
6. How Confirmation Works, Step by Step
   1.	Student fills the form → verifies OTP → enrolment is saved as Pending.
   2.	Fatima opens her admin dashboard and reviews pending enrolments.
   3.	Fatima clicks Accept on a student.
   4.	Student automatically gets an email confirming their seat — no phone call needed.
   5.	That student's status changes from Pending to Confirmed in Fatima's dashboard.
7. Removing Students & Handing Over the Account
   •	Removing a student: Fatima can click Remove on any enrolment (pending or confirmed) to delete it — for example, if someone cancels or doesn't respond. This frees up their seat immediately.
   •	Removing her own admin account: If Fatima ever wants to hand the business over to a new owner, she can go to her account settings and remove her own admin account. This immediately ends her access and reopens admin signup, so the new owner can create their own account and take over managing the site. This is a one-way action, so she'll be asked to confirm before it happens.
8. What This Website Does NOT Do (Out of Scope for Now)
   •	No online payment — enrolment and acceptance are free/manual review steps, not a paid checkout.
   •	No multiple admin accounts — only one admin (Fatima) can sign up and manage the site.
   •	No blog, testimonials, or extra marketing pages.
   •	No custom logo — business name is shown as text.
   •	Visitors cannot edit or remove their own enrolment once submitted (they'd need to contact Fatima directly for that).
9. Quick Summary for Fatima
   •	Visitors sign up online instead of calling → less phone work for you.
   •	You control course details, dates, and how many seats each batch has.
   •	You get a clean list of everyone who's signed up.
   •	You confirm seats with one click — the confirmation email is sent automatically.
   •	You can remove any student's entry if they cancel or don't respond.
   •	Full courses hide themselves — you don't have to remember to take them down.
   •	If you ever hand the business over, you can remove your own account so the new owner can sign up fresh.
